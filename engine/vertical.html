How it posts

One story, five posts, no extra work

Write onceClaude writes the documentary script, then cuts a 40-second version with a stronger hook.
RenderThe pipeline renders the 16:9 video, the 9:16 short and the 8 carousel slides from the same scenes.
ScheduleMetricool posts the short to TikTok, Reels, Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts, and the carousel to Instagram and Facebook.
LearnEach week Claude reads Metricool analytics and adjusts hooks, topics and posting times.

All scenes are drawn in code for this preview. Real posts use public-domain paintings, engravings and maps with the same layouts. The handle “historyhistory” is a placeholder until you choose the channel name.
